How to use this guide

In plain words: Orange is free software where you connect boxes with lines to make a small AI workflow. You do not type code. Each topic gives a story, then three levels from easy to harder.

Set up (10 minutes)

  1. Download Orange from orangedatamining.com and install it.
  2. Open Orange, choose Options → Add-ons, tick Text, Image Analytics, Network and Explain, then OK and restart.
  3. Open the Data tab here and download the files into one folder.

Train to build a legal chatbot

Scenario. A legal aid clinic in Windhoek gets the same questions every week: arrest rights, privacy, electronic signatures, and what to do after a cyber incident. You will train a chatbot that answers general questions from trusted texts and sends personal problems to a lawyer. Everything is built step by step, starting from the basics.

In plain words: A chatbot has three jobs. First, understand what the person is asking. Second, find the right trusted text. Third, answer safely and say "ask a lawyer" when it should. You will train each job yourself.

          Namibian legal frame (check before teaching)
          

          Reports from September 2026 say Namibia still has no dedicated cybercrime act or data protection act in force, although both bills are in progress. In the meantime, cases rely on laws such as the Electronic Transactions Act of 2019, the Communications Act and general criminal law. The Communications Regulatory Authority of Namibia (CRAN) runs the national incident response team, NAM-CSIRT. Confirm the latest status on official sources before you rely on it.
